What I believe

Stop fixing bugs.
Start removing bug classes.↗
  • blockingRules earn the right to block: a false-positive rate under 5% over a quarter, never straight to Block.
  • AIAI runs between rule evaluation and PR action, never in front of SAST.
  • severityPattern presence is not reachability. A “Critical” behind a network ACL is not Critical.
  • bountyTriage SLA matters more than bounty amount.

one of seven rules

Missing actor

Flags Ash changesets and queries built without an actor:. Without one, the resource’s policies may not run. Calls that pass a real actor are skipped.

ash-changeset-missing-actor.yaml ↗ 
rules:
  - id: ash-changeset-missing-actor
    languages: [elixir]
    severity: HIGH
    message: >
      Ash changeset or query executed without a valid `actor:` context.
      Nil or placeholder actors may bypass policy enforcement.
    patterns:
      - pattern-either:
          - pattern: Ash.Changeset.for_$ACTION($RESOURCE, $INPUT)
          - pattern: Ash.Query.for_read($RESOURCE, $ACTION)
      - pattern-not: Ash.Changeset.for_$ACTION($RESOURCE, $INPUT, actor: ($ACTOR & !nil))
      - pattern-not: Ash.Query.for_read($RESOURCE, $ACTION, actor: ($ACTOR & !nil))
