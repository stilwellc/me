Four Walls

A residence taken back to the studs, documented as found and as intended.

statusIn progress2024 – now
scopeFull residencepre-war railroad plan
roomsKitchen, living, dining, master bedroom, bedroom/office, bath6
updatedPhotos and plans current as ofSep 2026

Chapter I · as found

One long line of rooms with gray paint on every wall, maple strip floors, steam heat, and window AC units. Photographed at handover.

01 · kitchen

Galley kitchen, mid-plan.

Flat-panel cabinets, granite counters, gas range, and a pass-through to the dining room.

02 · living room

The long room on the entry side.

Crown molding, a cast-iron radiator on the party wall, and a through-wall AC unit.

03 · dining room

Between kitchen and living space.

04 · master bedroom

Closets, casings, and a ceiling fan.

Tall paneled window casings, two sliding-door closets, a ceiling fan, a radiator, and through-wall AC.

05 · bedroom / office

Second bedroom, used as the office.

At the rear of the plan. Pressed-tin ceiling, exposed steam riser, one tall window with a security grille.

06 · bath

The single bath.

Narrow vanity, glass-door shower, frosted double-hung window over the toilet.

Chapter II · as intended

The master bedroom’s double closet becomes one, widening the opening to the living room. The kitchen counter extends and squares off toward the dining room, and a built-in replaces the freestanding closet in the bedroom-office. All three keep the line of rooms continuous so light reaches its full length.
