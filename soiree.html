the problem

Most listings had no real date.

When Soirée started, fewer than half the scraped events carried a real date. Many sources post recurring or open-ended listings with none.

the build

Real dates first, placeholders marked ongoing.

Events with real dates sort first. Placeholder dates render as ongoing and are marked past, with history kept, once they vanish from their source.

the run

One scraper per source.

The feed read on Nov 15, 2026 carries 24 sources, among them the Whitney, the New Museum, the Brooklyn Botanic Garden, NJPAC, Barrow Mansion in Jersey City and the Hoboken Historical Museum. Each scraper has its own success-rate target and timeout.
