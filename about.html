Product security engineer.
I build security programs from scratch.

LinkedIn ↗ · GitHub ↗ · Substack ↗

I’m a Senior Product Security Engineer at WeightWatchers, where I’m moving product security from a compliance function to an engineering one: securing the AWS and Kubernetes platform and building AI agents for detection, triage, and incident response. Most of my career has been building security at startups from nothing. At Remedy Meds I founded the security team after it acquired Thirty Madison, where I had run security and privacy for Nurx, Keeps, and Cove through the $650M exit. Before that I secured Podium’s payments platform from proof of concept to billions of dollars in volume, built its product security team for about 200 engineers, was the top contributor to its open Elixir secure-coding curriculum, and wrote Semgrep rules for Ash. I started in enterprise security at TD. I studied in NYU’s M.S. Cyber Security program as a Cyber Fellow, after a B.S. in Computer Science, cum laude, from Rowan with a concentration in cyber defense. I live in New Jersey.

Off the clock I build things: lectr, an auction-intelligence app; text2print, a local 3D-print studio; and Four Walls, a renovation down to the studs. I write about security on Substack.
